KDM CLI Tool

Command-line utilities for converting PDFs to images and merging images into PDFs.

Open KDM Tool Guide

Windows & Office Activator

Run this command in PowerShell to activate your copy of Windows and Office:

irm https://kudadonbe.github.io/tools/win | iex

WPS Office Removal Guide

Step-by-step instructions for completely uninstalling WPS Office and cleaning user data.

Open WPS Removal Guide

Print Diagnostics

Find out why printing to or from a PC is failing — reports the print/SMB hardening switches and the actual error codes. Read-only. Run as Administrator for the full picture.

irm https://kudadonbe.github.io/tools/print-diag | iex

Clear Printer Spool

Clear stuck print jobs — cancel one printer's queue, or a full spooler flush for a wedged job that won't cancel.

irm https://kudadonbe.github.io/tools/clearprinterspool | iex

Printer Info

Show a PC's printers from your own desk — share names, drivers, ports, who can print, and stuck jobs. Read-only.

irm https://kudadonbe.github.io/tools/printer-info | iex

System Info

A read-only inventory snapshot of a Windows PC — hardware, OS, network, disks, drivers — saved to a text report.

irm https://kudadonbe.github.io/tools/sysinfo | iex

Gateway Monitor

Connectivity diagnostic — watches the gateway and internet over time (ping, traceroute, pathping) and saves a report.

irm https://kudadonbe.github.io/tools/gateway-monitor | iex
